My Hero - Gandalf

Interest Hero

When I first started watching the Lord of the Rings, Gandalf stood out to me as the old, grey beard, pointy hat, smokes pipe-weed, very tall guy. I think he was tall because he was around very small people like the hobbits. But he is really funny and very wise who guides Frodo. Furthermore, he values friendship more than anything material or power in the world. To learn more about the Lord of the Rings, check out my "Interest" page.

Actor
Sir Ian McKellen

Mithandir, also called Gandalf the Grey which later refer as Gandalf the White
Famous Qoute
