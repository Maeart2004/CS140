Kawasan Falls

The Visayas region is the home of approximately 20 million Filipinos representing 18.88% of the entire population of the Philippines. It is divided into 6 major islands with different languages, culture, and beautiful landscapes. Each of these islands are unique in traditions.

I love living in the Visayas region! I live in Iloilo City which is know as the city of love because not only does the Visayas located in the very center of the Philippines, the town which I live just exhibits charm and love from the tone of voice of the people and the culture. Lastly, I love the beautiful sceneries! It has many beautiful beaches and falls like the Kawasan falls located in Cebu! It is clear and you can see the reflection of the beautiful sky.
