Structural Tags(HEADER,NAV,MAIN,FOOTER)

-This is essential for defining the lay-out of a webpage. Developers also understand the structure and meaning of the content.

What goes in the Header?

-The header is used to understand the content and it includes navigational links.

What goes in the Nav? Can the Nav go in the header? Does it have to go in the header? Does the Nav contain links to another website?

What goes in the Main?

- It is the central part of the page where the core content, visuals. It is also found after the header and before the footer.

What goes in the footer?

-It goes at the very bottom and has many website links, address, contacts and many more important information for navigation within the website.
